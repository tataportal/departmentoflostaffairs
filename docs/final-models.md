# Final models — 14 October 2026

MARU and SHIBUI are the only available products. ANDON is under development; remaining displayed concepts are coming soon and carry no price.

Production sources: Department of Lost Affairs/3D/MARU.3mf and SHIBUI.3mf. The source hashes, component placement and full triangle counts are recorded in public/models/provenance.json. Print-bed positions and duplicate production instances are excluded. MARU assembly seating follows the accompanying MARU.blend: disc at 17.6 mm and shade at 20 mm. Source files are never changed.

Regenerate with Blender in factory/background mode using scripts/export_final_models.py -- /path/to/3D, then node scripts/optimize_models.mjs maru shibui. Compression preserves all triangles. The older export_models.py is for archived concept assemblies only.

The browser keeps saved Pebble light settings when migrating the product ID to MARU. The disc remains white and non-emissive; only the stones illuminate.
