DLA / About

Department of
Lost Affairs

Department of Lost Affairs is an office dedicated to investigating ideas, cultures, techniques, forms and stories that deserve to become objects.

Our background is in worldbuilding, digital twins and 3D design. We learned to read worlds as systems: to understand their codes, relationships, materials and rituals. Today, we use that same logic to create contemporary lighting and objects.

We do not copy references. We study them, translate them and give them a new form.

We investigate things. Occasionally, they become objects.
